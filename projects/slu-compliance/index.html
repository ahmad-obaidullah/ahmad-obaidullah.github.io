Audit-Ready Compliance Intelligence for Saint Louis University's International Students

The problem

SLU's international student data lived in two worlds that never touch: the internal CRM and the US government's SEVIS database. A thorough investigation confirmed zero direct matches between the systems via any identifier: the CRM's Reference_ID is never transmitted to SEVIS, and the CRM export contains no email or name fields at all. Every bridge strategy was attempted and documented; none worked.

What I did
  • Architected a PostgreSQL-based data infrastructure in Supabase: processed and deduplicated 34,341 raw records into a high-integrity 6,875-record master database, including correcting complex, Excel-corrupted date formats.
  • Engineered two core PostgreSQL views (vw_crm_analytics, vw_sevis_analytics) and 10 custom SQL queries feeding a 5-page interactive Looker Studio dashboard integrating 12 data sources.
  • Made the analytical call that defined the project: standalone analytics, analyzing each system independently with count-level reconciliation, after a documented bridge-strategy audit proved the identity gap.
The impact
  • The identity gap itself became a critical structural finding; resolving it is a primary recommendation of the report.
  • Deposit status is the primary predictor of enrollment: 82.7% conversion for deposited students vs 21.2% for non-deposited cohorts.
  • Translated 3,523 SEVIS records into operational intelligence, flagging high-risk cohorts (e.g., Nepal at a 15.6% fee-payment rate) against intake deadlines, enabling targeted outreach that minimizes end-of-cycle compliance backlogs.
  • Outcome: Star Performer badge, the team's top recognition. We didn't just complete the internship; we won it.
