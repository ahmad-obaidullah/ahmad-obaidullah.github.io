About

The analyst behind the numbers

I'm Ahmad, a data analyst based in Lahore, Pakistan. I recently earned the IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate and have since built end-to-end analytics work: a PostgreSQL pipeline that deduplicated 34,341 raw records into a 6,875-record master database for Saint Louis University, an AI workflow that cut A/B analysis time by ~83%, and a churn study that turned 541,909 transactions into a retention battle plan.

I love the full journey — data engineering and reconciliation, pattern hunting, and telling the story so a non-technical team knows exactly what to do next. I'm currently completing a No-Code AI Lab internship and sharpening prompt-engineering skills. I'm looking for a Junior Data Analyst role where business impact is the scorecard.

Off duty: books and yoga.
